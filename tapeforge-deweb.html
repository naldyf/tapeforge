Templates

One click loads a working NAND-only circuit. Every template is verified against its expected function.

How to wire

  1. Click a port (the small ring) on a gate output or an input pin.
  2. Click a port on a gate input or an output pin to complete the wire.
  3. Click an input pin to toggle it 0 / 1 and watch the outputs live.
  4. Drag a gate to move it. Click a wire to remove it.
  5. Right-click a gate or pin to delete it. Double-click a pin label to rename it.
How to use: 1. Pick a template on the left — or add your own gates. 2. Click a port, then click another port to wire them. 3. Press Verify circuit, check the price, export the netlist.

Exhaustive simulation: every input combination, checked against the expected function (up to 16 inputs = 65,536 cases; larger circuits are sampled deterministically).
